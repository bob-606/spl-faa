# EASA SPL to FAA Private Pilot Certificate

*A concise guide to obtaining a U.S. Private Pilot Certificate based on your EASA Sailplane Pilot Licence (SPL)*

---

## Legal Basis: 14 CFR § 61.75

> *"A person who holds a foreign pilot license **at the private pilot level or higher**... may apply for and be issued a private pilot certificate."*

### Why Your EASA SPL Qualifies

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| Is SPL at private pilot level? | **Yes** – EASA SPL allows private pilot privileges, carriage of passengers, and is ICAO-compliant |
| Does FAA accept it? | **Yes** – FAA Order 8900.1 explicitly lists EASA SPL as acceptable for conversion |
| What about LAPL(S)? | **No** – LAPL(S) is sub-ICAO and not eligible for conversion |

---

## Eligibility Requirements

| Requirement | Detail |
|-------------|--------|
| Valid foreign licence | EASA SPL (private pilot level or higher) |
| No existing U.S. certificate | Cannot hold a U.S. pilot certificate |
| Medical certificate | EASA Part-MED Class 2 **or** FAA Part 67 |
| English proficiency | Must read, speak, write, and understand English |
| ICAO compliance | No limitation stating you have not met ICAO standards |

---

## What You Will Receive

| Feature | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| Certificate | U.S. Private Pilot Certificate (Foreign-Based) |
| Rating | Glider category |
| Validity | Tied to your EASA SPL – if SPL is invalid, FAA certificate becomes invalid |
| Privileges | Private pilot in U.S.-registered (N-number) aircraft |

---

## Step-by-Step Process

### Step 1: Verify Your Licence (45-90 days)

**Via IACRA (Preferred):**
1. Register at `iacra.faa.gov`
2. Select **"START FOREIGN VERIFICATION PROCESS"**
3. Upload your EASA SPL

**By Mail:**
- Complete **FAA Form AC 8060-71**
- Mail to: FAA Airmen Certification Branch AFB-720, P.O. Box 25082, Oklahoma City, OK 73125

> Verification letter is valid for **6 months**.

### Step 2: Complete FAA Form 8710-1

Print from IACRA or download from FAA website.

### Step 3: Schedule In-Person Appointment

**Option A: FAA FSDO (U.S.)**
- Contact FSDO upon receipt of verification letter
- Allow **≥2 weeks** for appointment

**Option B: DPE-FPE (Europe)**
- Search `designee.faa.gov` for **DPE-FPE**
- Some flight schools have contracted DPE-FPEs

### Step 4: Attend Appointment

**Bring originals:**
- [ ] Verification of Authenticity Letter
- [ ] FAA Form 8710-1
- [ ] EASA SPL
- [ ] Medical certificate (EASA Part-MED Class 2 or FAA)
- [ ] Passport

### Step 5: Receive Certificate

- **Temporary certificate** issued on the spot
- **Permanent plastic card** mailed later by FAA

---

## Limitations

| Limitation | Explanation |
|------------|-------------|
| Tied to foreign licence | Valid only while EASA SPL remains valid |
| Private pilot only | No commercial privileges |
| No upgrade path | Cannot be used for Commercial Pilot Certificate without additional FAA testing |
| Foreign limitations apply | Any limitations on your SPL carry over |

---

## After Issuance – Maintenance

| Requirement | Frequency |
|-------------|-----------|
| Flight Review | Every 24 months (14 CFR § 61.56) |
| Medical | Must remain valid (EASA or FAA) |
| Foreign licence | Must remain valid |
| Currency | FAA recency requirements (14 CFR § 61.57) |
